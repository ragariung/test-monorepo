# PRAXIS Investa Syariah

**Category:** Sharia unit-linked (`investment`) · **Slug:** `praxis-investa-syariah` · **Badge:** Prinsip Syariah (Sharia Principle)

## Market pattern this represents

Modeled on sharia-compliant unit-linked products offered by AXA Mandiri Syariah, Allianz Syariah, and Prudential Syariah in Indonesia. The defining structural pattern across all of them: a *tabarru'* (mutual-help/donation) fund pays the protection benefit instead of a conventional insurer-underwritten risk pool, contributions are invested only in sharia-compliant instruments (sukuk, sharia equities), and a Dewan Pengawas Syariah (DPS / Sharia Supervisory Board) oversees compliance — all mirrored here.

## Why these numbers

- **Entry age 18–55**: a fairly standard unit-linked entry window — wider at the young end than the CI/legacy products since unit-linked protection is typically underwritten more leniently than pure critical-illness cover, but capped at 55 reflecting the investment-horizon assumptions unit-linked products depend on.
- **Sum assured Rp150 million – Rp4 miliar**: mid-range relative to the rest of the catalogue, consistent with unit-linked products in this market being positioned between mass-market protection and affluent legacy planning.
- **Payment terms 10/15/20 years only**: unit-linked/investment products in this market are rarely sold on very short terms, since the investment-growth value proposition needs a longer accumulation runway to be meaningful (similar reasoning to the CI product's term restriction, for a different reason).
- **80-year coverage duration**: a long horizon appropriate for a product that pairs lifetime-style protection with a long-run investment component, shorter than the pure whole-life products' 99-year horizon since it's not marketed purely as a legacy vehicle.
- **Tabarru' + Wakalah bil Ujrah structure**: the two contract terms actually used industry-wide in Indonesian sharia insurance — tabarru' for the mutual-protection fund, wakalah bil ujrah for the fee arrangement under which the insurer manages the fund. Included here as authentic terminology, not fabricated vocabulary.
- **Underwriting-surplus sharing**: a real, distinguishing feature of sharia insurance broadly — because the risk pool is member-owned (mutual), a healthy claims year can result in a surplus shared back with participants, unlike conventional insurance where underwriting profit stays with the insurer.

**Coverage table:** sharia death benefit → 100% sum assured + investment account balance, managed transparently in a segregated tabarru' account.

**Eligibility:** open to all Indonesian residents regardless of background; entry age 18–55.
