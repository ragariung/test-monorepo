# PRAXIS Cendekia

**Category:** Education endowment (`education`) · **Slug:** `praxis-cendekia` · **Badge:** Garansi Sekolah (School Guarantee)

## Market pattern this represents

Modeled on "dwiguna pendidikan" (dual-purpose education endowment) products common to AXA and Prudential's Indonesian lineups: a savings-linked life policy where guaranteed cash payouts are timed to a child's school milestones (entering middle school, high school, university), and — critically — a life-insurance component ensures the payout schedule continues uninterrupted even if the parent (policyholder) passes away before the schedule completes.

## Why these numbers

- **Policyholder age 21–50, insured child age 30 days–10 years**: matches how education-endowment products are always structured around two lives (paying parent + young child), unlike every other PRAXIS product which insures one adult life. The parent age floor (21) reflects legal-adult/parental age; the child age ceiling (10) reflects that the product needs enough runway before university-age payouts to make the endowment structure meaningful.
- **Sum assured Rp150 million – Rp2 miliar**: a lower ceiling than the pure protection products, consistent with education-endowment products being sized to actual projected tuition costs rather than open-ended wealth/estate goals.
- **22-year coverage duration**: sized to span from a newborn/young child through university graduation — a purpose-built duration rather than the "to age 99" pattern used by the whole-life products.
- **Payment terms 5/10 years only**: education products are commonly sold as shorter, front-loaded payment plans so the policy is fully paid up well before the staged payouts begin.
- **Premium waiver + continued payout on parent's death**: this is the defining, must-have feature of the category across every Indonesian insurer offering education plans — without it, the product is just a generic endowment, not an education plan.

**Key benefits:** guaranteed staged payouts at each school milestone (disbursed when the child turns 12, 15, and 18); dual protection — if the parent passes away, remaining premiums are waived and the education fund still pays out on schedule.

**Coverage table:** university-entry milestone payout → 50% of total sum assured, disbursed exactly when the child turns 18.

**Eligibility:** policyholder (parent) age 21–50; insured (child) age 30 days–10 years.
