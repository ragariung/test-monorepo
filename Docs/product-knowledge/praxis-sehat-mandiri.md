# PRAXIS Sehat Mandiri

**Category:** Critical illness (`critical-illness`) · **Slug:** `praxis-sehat-mandiri` · **Badge:** Proteksi Kritis (Critical Protection)

## Market pattern this represents

Modeled on standalone critical-illness (CI) plans and CI riders common across AXA, AIA, and Allianz in Indonesia. The recurring structure: cover a large, standardized list of conditions (typically 50–70+, e.g. cancer, stroke, heart attack) at multiple severity stages, pay out as a lump sum on diagnosis (not against hospital bills), and waive future premiums once a major claim is paid. AIA and AXA in particular popularized the "early-stage + late-stage" split payout structure this product uses.

## Why these numbers

- **68 covered conditions**: sits squarely in the typical range (50–70+) that Indonesian CI products advertise; a round, market-typical number rather than an exact insurer's published count.
- **Entry age 20–55**: narrower than whole-life products, reflecting that CI underwriting is more sensitive to age-related health risk — insurers commonly cap CI entry lower than pure death-benefit products.
- **Sum assured Rp200 million – Rp3 miliar**: higher floor than the base life product (Rp200 million vs. Rp100 million), reflecting that CI lump sums need to meaningfully cover treatment + income replacement, not just a baseline payout.
- **Payment terms 10/15/20 years only (no 5-year option)**: CI products in this market are rarely sold on very short pay terms, since the risk pool assumptions favor longer accumulation periods.
- **90-day waiting period**: a near-universal feature of CI products industry-wide, to prevent adverse selection (buying the policy right after a diagnosis).
- **50%/100% staged payout**: mirrors the standard "early stage pays partial, advanced stage pays remainder" structure used by AXA and AIA CI plans.

**Key benefits:** protection across 68 critical conditions (early/mid/terminal stage); lump-sum cash payout (no need to wait for hospital bills to be settled); premium waiver after a critical-condition claim is approved; partner hospital network across Indonesia, Malaysia, and Singapore.

**Coverage table:** early-stage cancer / minor cardiac surgery → 50% SA (claimable up to twice for different conditions); heart attack / malignant cancer / stroke → 100% remaining SA; post-hospitalization recovery allowance → Rp15,000,000/incident (max once per policy year).

**Eligibility:** entry age 20–55; 90-day waiting period from policy issuance; no prior critical-illness diagnosis history.

**Documents:** RIPLAY; list of 68 covered conditions with medical diagnosis criteria.
