# PRAXIS Jiwa Utama

**Category:** Whole life protection (`life`) · **Slug:** `praxis-jiwa-utama` · **Badge:** Paling Populer (Most Popular)

## Market pattern this represents

Modeled on the general structure of whole-life protection products in the Indonesian market — e.g. Allianz's individual life portfolio (LegacyPro-style lifetime coverage), Prudential's PRUMapan (limited-pay, lifetime term), and AIA's whole-life plans. The common shape: pay premiums for a fixed, shorter number of years; stay covered for life (or to a very old age like 99). This is consistently the flagship/anchor product in each insurer's individual life lineup, which is why it's the "Most Popular" badge holder here too.

## Why these numbers

- **Entry age 18–60**: standard adult working-age entry window seen across the market; upper bound of 60 reflects typical maximum-entry-age underwriting caps for whole-life products (vs. term life, which sometimes allows older entry).
- **Sum assured Rp100 million – Rp5 miliar**: spans mass-market (Rp100 million floor, roughly the minimum seen for standalone life plans) through affluent segment (Rp5 miliar), matching the wide band insurers typically publish to serve both first-time buyers and high-net-worth clients on the same product.
- **Payment terms 5/10/15/20 years**: the standard "limited-pay" menu — pay off the policy faster (5 years) for a higher annual premium, or spread it out (20 years) for lower annual cost, both ending in lifetime coverage. This mirrors Prudential PRUMapan's limited-payment structuring.
- **Coverage to age 99**: the conventional "whole life" horizon used across Indonesian insurers instead of a literal unbounded term.
- **200% accidental death benefit**: a very common rider-equivalent built into base whole-life plans in this market — doubling the payout for accidental (vs. natural/illness) death.

**Key benefits:** 100% cash sum assured (tax-free to beneficiary); flexible payment term choice; flat premium for the chosen term (no age-inflation increases); 24/7 digital claims with dedicated claims officer.

**Coverage table:** natural/illness death → 100% SA; accidental death → 200% SA; optional waiver-of-premium rider on total permanent disability/critical condition; guaranteed cash value if the insured survives to 99.

**Eligibility:** Indonesian citizens or valid KITAS/KITAP holders; entry age 18–60 (nearest birthday); standard (non-substandard) health at initial declaration; valid ID + registered Indonesian bank account.

**Documents:** RIPLAY (general summary); official 2026 brochure; standard policy terms & exclusions.
