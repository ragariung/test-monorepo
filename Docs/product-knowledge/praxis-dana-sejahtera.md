# PRAXIS Dana Sejahtera

**Category:** Savings/endowment (`savings`) · **Slug:** `praxis-dana-sejahtera` · **Badge:** Garansi Bonus (Guaranteed Bonus)

## Market pattern this represents

Modeled on conservative, guaranteed-return endowment savings plans offered alongside unit-linked products across the Indonesian market — positioned deliberately as the "safe, boring, predictable" alternative for buyers who want life cover plus a savings component, without market-linked investment risk. This is a recurring product tier insurers maintain specifically to compete with, and be more predictable than, term deposits and unit-linked plans.

## Why these numbers

- **Entry age 20–60**: a broad, mass-market-friendly window (wider than the CI or legacy products), consistent with this being positioned as an accessible, low-risk savings vehicle rather than a specialist or affluent-segment product.
- **Sum assured Rp100 million – Rp3 miliar**: same floor as the base life product (Rp100 million), since this product's marketing angle is accessibility, not scale — the ceiling is intentionally lower than the pure protection or legacy products because the primary value here is the savings return, not a large death benefit.
- **20-year fixed coverage/maturity duration**: endowment savings products in this market are almost always sold with a single fixed maturity horizon (unlike whole-life's "to age 99"), since the entire point is a defined maturity date and return.
- **110% premium return at maturity**: a deliberately conservative, clearly-stated guaranteed return figure — matching how conservative endowment products differentiate themselves from unit-linked plans by promising a fixed, contractual number instead of a market-dependent projection.
- **Loyalty bonus every 3 years**: a common retention mechanic in this product category, rewarding policyholders who keep paying without lapsing.
